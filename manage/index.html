Manage

Manage contains the retail master data used throughout IMS. In IMS 9.4.4, the Supplier master also holds VAT registration and VAT Account No. when VAT is enabled.

Manage menu

ModuleScope
SuppliersMaintain supplier master data, brands, integration/JIT settings, cost settings, custom attributes and mailing lists.
StoresMaintain Store/warehouse definitions, operational settings, financial mapping, POS terminals, Sections and Store attributes.
Sales Men/LadiesMaintain salesperson records, active state and the Stores in which each salesperson can sell.
ItemsMaintain the fashion item master including Style, Color, Size, pricing, classification, supplier/brand, online details and item images.
Service ItemsMaintain non-stock/service transaction items such as fees, coupons, credit notes and service charges.
CategoriesMaintain Category and Subcategory hierarchy used by items and reporting.
Product GroupsMaintain Product Group and Subgroup hierarchy.
Age RangesMaintain merchandise age-range classifications.
GendersMaintain merchandise gender classifications.
Statistical PeriodsMaintain reporting/merchandise periods by Year and Season.
Sale SeasonsDefine seasonal markdown policies, dates and item price changes.
Special OffersConfigure discount, free-item, cashback, bundle and step-discount promotions with Store/client/item scope.
Give Away PromotionsMaintain Give Away voucher/promotion definitions and their Store/client criteria.
Discount CouponsConfigure single or generated discount coupons, discount/amount rules, validity and Store scope.
Loyalty Rewards OffersConfigure loyalty-reward promotional offers.
Lottery OffersConfigure lottery offer dates, trigger amount, participating Stores and draw date.
VIP CustomersMaintain VIP/customer account information, category, contact data, discount/credit settings and related customer identity.
Email ContactsMaintain reusable email contacts.
Email GroupsOrganize reusable email recipients into groups.
Loyalty Rewards TiersMaintain loyalty tier definitions used by the rewards program.
Special CardsSearch and review issued cards, customer/card details, balance and card transactions.
Special Cards ProfilesDefine card profiles including validity, one-time use, redeem payment type, funding item and recharge discount.

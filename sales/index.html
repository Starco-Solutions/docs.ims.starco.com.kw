Sales

Sales contains the five direct POS toolbar functions used for sales, returns, item lookup, POS shifts, cash drawer access and transaction administration. IMS 9.4.4 also exposes VAT Base, VAT % and VAT Amount where VAT is enabled.

Sales toolbar

ButtonPurpose
Sales TransactionsOpen the main POS transaction screen for sales, returns, exchanges, payments, customer/loyalty processing and related transaction workflows.
Items SearchSearch the IMS item master and retail availability information while working at the POS.
Open/Close POSOpen and close POS shifts, select active card machines, record opening/closing cash and permanently close the POS business day.
Open DrawerOpen the connected POS cash drawer when the user's policy allows it.
Transactions ExpertReview and administer existing sales and Stock Transfer transactions using the transaction-management tools available to the user's policy.

POS state on the main screen

The toolbar reflects the current POS state. When the POS is closed, the button reads Open POS and Sales Transactions is unavailable. After the POS is opened, the button changes to Close POS and the main screen shows the active POS date and selected bank/card machines.

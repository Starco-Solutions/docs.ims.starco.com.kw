Reports

The Reports menu brings together the operational, sales, customer, profitability, inventory-performance and purchasing reports used across IMS.

Reports menu

ModuleScope
Cash ReportReview POS cash activity and daily cash totals by Store/POS and business date.
Store Sales PaymentsAnalyze Store sales by payment type, transaction type and receipt when required.
Store Sales ReportReview Store sales performance, Cash/Credit activity, refunds, traffic and conversion measures.
Sales Men/Ladies SalesAnalyze Sales Man/Lady performance, quantities, amounts, invoices, ATV and UPT.
Clients HistoryReview customer purchase history by date, Store, category, client and transaction detail.
Star Rewards HistoryReview Star Rewards customer account, points/wallet information and reward transactions.
Clients VisitsAnalyze customer visits/new clients, spend and percentage measures by time or Store grouping.
Gross IncomeAnalyze inventory, sales, cost, markup, gross profit and GPM by Supplier or Store.
Gift Vouchers SummarySummarize issued, expired, canceled, net and redeemed gift-voucher amounts.
Main Sales ReportFlexible multi-dimensional sales analysis with extensive filters, Group By options, KPIs and traffic/conversion support.
Sell Through ReportAnalyze received stock, net sales, remaining stock, sell-through, sales mix, ADR and related merchandise KPIs.
Purchasing ReportAnalyze purchasing quantities, landed/local cost, retail value, expenses, markup and merchandise dimensions.
User-Defined ReportsRun reports created through Custom Reports and made available to authorized IMS users.
